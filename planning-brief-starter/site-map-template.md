# Site Map Template

## Site Purpose
Help people learn basic and advanced training techniques from your my videos and request private sessions. 

## Primary Audience
People who want coaching on beginner level to advanced techniques either online or in-person. 

## Primary User Tasks

1. Request Sessions
2. Watch Tutorials
3. Explore Trainer Background 

## Proposed Site Map

- Home
  - Purpose: The page should be easy to read and highlight the name of the Company
  - Key content: Bulletproof Personal Training, Highlight Video and Navigation 
- Media
  - Purpose: Allow traffic to view some content and tutorials
  - Key content: Free tutorials and locked tutorials 
- Services
  - Purpose: Book personal training sessions and unlock advanced videos
  - Key content: Free tutorials for beginners and a calendar to book sessions 
- About
  - Purpose: Trainer background 
  - Key content: Intro video and links to other platforms

## Content Risks to Resolve

1. Video hosting
2. Calendly link or Google meet instead of requesting sessions
3. Copyright
